## 🎬 Word-for-Word Presenter Script

### [0:00 - 0:30] Introduction & The Problem (30 Seconds)
**Visual on Screen:** Presentation Title Slide showing "TERA PULSE - SIH26053", then transition to Slide 2 (Problem Definition). Presenter webcam visible in corner.

> *"Respected Evaluators and Jury Members, greetings from Team TERA PULSE. We are presenting our solution for Problem Statement SIH26053: Adaptive Variable Resolution 2.5D LiDAR Mapping for Dynamic Environment Perception.*
>
> *In autonomous tactical defense and off-road UGV navigation, vehicles must perceive complex terrain in real time. However, modern 3D LiDAR sensors stream millions of points per second. Processing uniform 3D voxel grids creates massive memory bottlenecks and latency that overwhelm embedded edge computers. Conversely, standard 2D occupancy grids discard elevation entirely, leaving vehicles blind to steep curbs, ditches, and overhangs. Today, we demonstrate how TERA PULSE eliminates this bottleneck."*

---

### [0:30 - 1:00] The Proposed Architecture (30 Seconds)
**Visual on Screen:** Slide 4 (Foveated Vision Principle) showing concentric distance rings (0.25m, 0.75m, 2.0m).

> *"Our core innovation is a biomimetic foveated mapping engine inspired by human eyesight. Instead of wasting uniform high resolution on distant empty space, our system dynamically splits the environment into three concentric bands:*
>
> *In the near hazard zone from zero to 10 meters, we use a fine zero-point-two-five meter grid for millimeter-level obstacle clearance and steering. In the middle tactical corridor from 10 to 30 meters, we allocate zero-point-seven-five meter cells. Beyond 30 meters, we use coarse two-meter cells to anchor regional boundaries.*
>
> *Crucially, every single cell maintains full 2.5D elevation layers, terrain roughness, and semantic classifications—giving us 3D awareness with 2D computational efficiency."*

## 💡 Practical Recording Tips for the Team

1. **Clean Terminal:** Run `cls` before starting the recording so the terminal is completely clean.
2. **Pre-check Resolution:** Set your display resolution to 1920x1080 (100% scaling) so fonts and dashboard details are crisp.
3. **Sound Check:** Use a dedicated headset or USB mic; avoid echo or background fan noise.
4. **Rehearsal:** Practice the speech twice with a stopwatch to ensure you finish between 2 minutes 50 seconds and 3 minutes flat!
